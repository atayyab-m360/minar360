For organizations

From download to live, step by step

You've decided to bring your organization to Minar360 — here's exactly how to get your profile live.

Download→ Create→ Go Live
1

Download Minar360

Get the app from the App Store on your phone or tablet. (Android via Google Play is on the way.)

2

Create an account

Sign up with your email, then choose "Organization" as your account type.

3

Create your organization profile

Add your organization's name, category (masjid, nonprofit, or business), address, and contact details.

4

Complete your profile

Add a logo, a short description, and your hours — this is what your community sees first.

5

Add prayer & iqamah times

Masjids can publish daily prayer and iqamah times directly to their profile, visible to anyone who follows you.

6

Create your first announcement

Share news, updates, or reminders with your followers — it lands right in their feed.

7

Create your first event

Add a date, time, and location. Choose whether it's free or ticketed, and publish.

8

Set up donations

Connect a secure payment processor so your organization can start accepting donations — funds go directly to you, Minar360 never holds them.

9

Invite your community to follow

Share your Minar360 profile link with your community so your events and updates start reaching them.

Careers & volunteering

Grow the Minar360 network

As Minar360 grows city by city, we're building a network of representatives across the country to help bring their local communities on board.

Why we're building this

Minar360 is still early — and reaching every masjid, nonprofit, and Muslim-owned business in a city takes someone who already knows that community. As we expand, we're looking for volunteers who want to help introduce Minar360 to their own local network: reaching out to organizations, answering questions, and helping them get set up.

📍

Representatives across the USA

Whether you're in a city where Minar360 already has a presence or one we haven't reached yet, we'd love to hear from you. This is a volunteer role for now — a way to help shape Minar360 in your community as we grow.
